Token flywheelHow a colony grows

The loop

More holders grow the colony treasury. A bigger treasury hatches more ants, and more ants means more trading capacity. When trades close in profit, part of it buys the token back, which can attract more holders.

The loop only turns when trades are profitable. Losing trades shrink the treasury and the colony with it. Nothing about the flywheel is guaranteed.
